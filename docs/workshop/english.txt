[h1]Combat Solver[/h1]

Combat Solver is a single-player combat route planner for Slay the Spire 2. Once combat begins, it simulates your hand, piles, enemy intents, potions, relics, and future turns in the background to find a recommended line and projected HP loss. Follow the advice yourself, play one turn, or turn on auto-play to handle the fight.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Fast multi-turn engine[/b]: Simulates future draws, shuffles, enemy intents, relics, and powers to optimize survival and total combat HP loss rather than immediate damage alone.
[*][b]Clear routes & flexible control[/b]: Displays card plays, targets, choices, potions, key relic triggers, and projected HP loss/healing by turn. Review only, click [b]Play turn[/b], or toggle [b]Auto: On[/b] (with optional auto-enable each combat). You can also [b]Stop search[/b], [b]Use candidate[/b], [b]Freeze route[/b], or toggle the overlay anytime with Ctrl+F9.
[*][b]Per-potion policies & HP thresholds[/b]: Open [b]Potions[/b] to set each held potion to Smart use, Always use, or Disabled / Protected, and customize each potion's [b]HP threshold[/b] directly (saved per potion type; newly acquired potions default to Smart use).
[*][b]Relic counter, growth & boss policies[/b]: Target ending counters, extra HP allowances, and priorities (1–3) for cyclic relics such as Happy Flower, Pendulum, Pen Nib, and Meat on the Bone; configure HP allowances for out-of-combat scaling cards, toggle [b]Ignore out-of-combat gains[/b], cap Brightest Flame max-HP cost, and pick Progression first or Minimize HP loss for Act 1/2 and Final bosses.
[*][b]Native choices & route reuse[/b]: Turn-start and draw/discard choices keep the game's native screens and animations; unchanged states reuse the existing route across turns and only recalculate when the live state diverges.
[*][b]Built-in bug reports[/b]: Submit issues from [b]Settings > Reports > Upload report[/b] (with automatic classification), or use [b]Export report[/b] to save a local archive.
[/list]

[h2]Getting started[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Subscribe to and enable the required RitsuLib mod.
[*]Enable Combat Solver and enter a single-player combat.
[*]Wait for the opening draw and turn-start effects; calculation starts automatically by default.
[*]Follow the suggested plays, click [b]Play turn[/b], or toggle [b]Auto: On[/b].
[/olist]

[h2]Requirements and compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*]Requires RitsuLib 0.6.11 or later.
[*]Supports Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1 single-player Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes (compatible with 0.113.0).
[*]Single-player combat only; multiplayer and gameplay-altering content mods are not supported.
[*]Background search never modifies the live game's RNG.
[*]Search is bounded by your solve-time limit. The displayed route is the best found within that budget, not a guarantee of a mathematically optimal solution.
[/list]

[h2]Performance[/h2]

The redesigned high-speed, low-memory engine works out of the box with no memory-management tweaks needed. Normal fights typically resolve almost instantly, while complex fights automatically deepen up to your [b]Max combat solve time[/b] (default 4 s, configurable 1–15 s). [b]Max parallel threads[/b] scales automatically to your CPU and can be tuned or set to single-threaded in Settings.

[h2]Online presence[/h2]

Online presence is enabled by default (sharing online status, anonymous run statistics, and eligible Ascension 10 Act 3 boss flawless routes) and can be turned off anytime under [b]Settings > General > Online presence[/b].
