# CombatSolver 1.0.1

## 简体中文

CombatSolver 1.0.1 针对 1.0.0 新一代引擎重点修复策略生效链路与多类战斗结算重算，支持《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.1 单人标准、恐惧与极速模式（兼容 0.113.0）。请确保同时启用 RitsuLib 0.6.11 或更高版本。

- **恢复「遗物计数策略」与「成长策略」完整生效**：修复升级 1.0.0 新引擎后，「遗物计数策略」中的循环计数遗物目标控制、带骨肉等战后遗物回血权衡，以及「成长策略」中全部长期成长收益额度、「不考虑局外收益」开关与「限制至亮之焰的最大生命消耗」未接入路线搜索的问题；同时修复编辑成长数值后直接关闭或切换面板可能未保存，以及非战斗状态下修改成长与遗物设置无法保留的问题，并在未启用相关策略时保持原版极速搜索表现。
- **大幅消除高风险场景预测偏差与重算**：基于 1.0.0 本地实机对局日志与全量高风险场景排查，系统修复全部 6 位角色在标准、恐惧与极速模式下的大量结算细节偏差——涵盖多段攻击引发牌堆变化时的目标与手牌状态、无实体／缓冲与减伤遗物的先后结算及小数余伤误耗缓冲、临时属性增减与回合末退场顺序、批量变牌与状态牌生成时的减费时机、回溯上回合攻击牌保护、愚者旋律首打触发与能力能量收益等复杂互动，显著降低高风险战斗中的意外重算与路线偏差。
- **修复隐式全选与多张牌置顶顺序**：对齐游戏原版在合资格卡牌数不超过必选张数时直接按牌堆顺序自动选取的规则，并修正将多张手牌放回抽牌堆顶时的排列顺序，减少手动与全自动执行过程中的选牌错位。

## English

CombatSolver 1.0.1 focuses on restoring strategy policy integration and eliminating recalculations discovered after the 1.0.0 engine overhaul. It supports single-player Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes in Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1 (compatible with 0.113.0). Please ensure RitsuLib 0.6.11 or later is enabled.

- **Restored Full Integration for Relic Counters & Growth Policies**: Fixed gaps after the 1.0.0 engine transition so counter-relic targets and post-combat healing trade-offs (such as Meat on the Bone) under **Relic counters**, as well as all out-of-combat gain allowances, **Ignore out-of-combat gains**, and **Limit Brightest Flame max HP cost** under **Growth**, once again guide route search accurately. Also fixed pending growth inputs being lost when switching or closing panels directly, and ensured changes made out of combat persist properly without slowing searches when these policies are inactive.
- **Broad Reduction in High-Risk Prediction Mismatches & Replans**: Based on local 1.0.0 playtest logs and high-risk differential sweeps, resolved dozens of edge-case combat discrepancies across all 6 characters in Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes—including pile shifts during multi-hit attacks, Intangible/Buffer and damage-cap ordering (plus fractional remaining damage not consuming Buffer), temporary stat application and turn-end retirement order, batch card transforms and Status-generation cost reductions, previous-turn attack tracking protection, Fool first-melody repeat triggers, and energy power payouts—greatly reducing unplanned replans in complex fights.
- **Fixed Implicit Auto-Select & Multi-Card Top-of-Draw Ordering**: Aligned mandatory multi-card selection with vanilla pile-order auto-selection when eligible cards do not exceed required picks, and corrected top-of-draw ordering when placing multiple hand cards back on the draw pile—preventing selection misalignment during manual and auto-play execution.
