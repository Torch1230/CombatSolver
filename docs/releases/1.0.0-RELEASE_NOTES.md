# CombatSolver 1.0.0

## 简体中文

CombatSolver 1.0.0 带来全新一代高速战斗求解引擎，现已支持《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.1 单人标准、恐惧与极速模式。

请确保同时启用 RitsuLib 0.6.11 或更高版本。

- **全新高速战斗求解引擎**：全面重构战斗模拟与路线搜索核心，求解速度大幅提升，内存占用与界面卡顿大幅降低；即使是复杂的多回合战斗，也能极快生成推荐路线。随着内存占用大幅降低，旧版的内存管理设置与手动释放内存按钮已不再需要并已移除，开箱即用。
- **消除首场战斗启动卡顿**：优化游戏启动后的初始化流程，消除进入本局第一场战斗时的明显卡顿。
- **重做智能药水决策与逐瓶省血阈值**：在「药水策略」面板中直接为当前持有的每瓶药水开放独立的「省血阈值」设置，用直观透明的等价省血标准替代旧版隐藏换算规则；按药水类型自动保存，支持单瓶恢复「默认」或一键「重置阈值」，修改后立即重新计算路线。
- **v0.113.1 适配与全角色战斗准确度全面提升**：同步《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.1 规则，全面梳理并修复全部 6 位角色的卡牌、遗物、能力、药水与怪物结算细节，大幅减少分步执行与全自动战斗中因预测偏差导致的意外重算。

## English

CombatSolver 1.0.0 introduces a brand-new high-speed combat engine, supporting single-player Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes in Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1.

Please make sure RitsuLib 0.6.11 or later is enabled.

- **Brand-New High-Speed Combat Engine**: Rebuilt combat simulation and route search from the ground up for dramatically faster solving, drastically lower memory usage, and smoother gameplay—generating routes nearly instantaneously even in complex multi-turn fights. Because the new engine uses far less memory by design, the old memory-management settings and manual memory-release button are no longer needed and have been removed.
- **Eliminated First-Combat Startup Stutter**: Improved startup initialization so entering your first combat of a session no longer causes a noticeable freeze or hitch.
- **Rebuilt Potion Decisions & Per-Potion HP Thresholds**: Added per-potion **HP threshold** controls directly inside the **Potions** panel, replacing hidden scaling rules with clear, predictable HP-saving thresholds. Settings are saved per potion type, support per-potion **Default** and one-click **Reset HP**, and recalculate immediately when changed.
- **v0.113.1 Compatibility & Broad Combat Accuracy Overhaul**: Updated for Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1 with sweeping rule and resolution fixes across all 6 characters' cards, relics, powers, potions, and monsters—substantially reducing prediction mismatches and unexpected mid-combat recalculations during manual and auto-play execution.
