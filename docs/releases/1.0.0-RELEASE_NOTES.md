# CombatSolver 1.0.0

## 简体中文

CombatSolver 1.0.0 带来全新高速战斗求解引擎，支持《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.1 单人标准、恐惧与极速模式（兼容 0.113.0）。请确保同时启用 RitsuLib 0.6.11 或更高版本。

- **全新高速引擎与精简性能设置**：全面重构战斗模拟与路线搜索核心，普通战斗几乎瞬间出解，复杂战斗会在设定时限内自动逐级深化；内存占用与游玩卡顿大幅降低。旧版繁杂的档位预设、内存管理选项与手动清理按钮已不再需要并全面移除，性能页现仅保留「战斗最大求解时长（默认 4 秒）」与「最大并发线程数」，开箱即用。
- **消除首场战斗启动卡顿**：优化游戏启动后的初始化与界面准备流程，进入本局第一场战斗时不再出现明显卡顿。
- **重做智能药水决策与逐瓶省血阈值**：在「药水策略」面板中直接为当前持有的每瓶可用药水提供独立的「省血阈值」，以直观的等价省血标准替代旧版隐藏换算规则；按药水类型自动保存，支持单瓶恢复「默认」与一键「重置阈值」，修改后立即重新计算路线。
- **策略体验与路线显示优化**：加强 Boss 战、精英战与多回合战斗的路线规划及用药权衡；同时持有多个同周期但计数错位的遗物（如摆动球与开心小花）时自动按优先级协调目标，避免无谓拖长战斗；修复敌人因灾厄或回合末效果阵亡时，末回合路线战损显示不准确的问题。
- **完整适配 v0.113.1 与全角色准确度提升**：同步《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.1 规则，全面梳理并修复全部 6 位角色在标准、恐惧与极速模式下的卡牌、遗物、能力、药水与怪物结算细节，显著减少手操与全自动执行时的预测偏差与计划外重算。

## English

CombatSolver 1.0.0 introduces a brand-new high-speed combat engine, supporting single-player Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes in Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1 (compatible with 0.113.0). Please make sure RitsuLib 0.6.11 or later is enabled.

- **Brand-New High-Speed Engine & Streamlined Performance Settings**: Rebuilt combat simulation and route search from the ground up for drastically lower memory usage and much smoother gameplay. Normal fights resolve almost instantly, while complex fights automatically deepen up to your time cap. Legacy presets, memory-management toggles, and manual cleanup buttons are no longer needed and have been removed—Performance settings now keep only **Max combat solve time** (default 4 s) and **Max parallel threads**, ready out of the box.
- **Eliminated First-Combat Startup Stutter**: Improved startup initialization so entering your first combat of a session no longer causes a noticeable freeze or hitch.
- **Rebuilt Smart Potion Decisions & Per-Potion HP Thresholds**: Added per-potion **HP threshold** controls directly inside the **Potions** panel, replacing hidden scaling rules with clear equivalent-HP thresholds. Settings are saved per potion type, support per-potion **Default** and one-click **Reset HP**, and recalculate immediately when changed.
- **Smarter Combat Strategies & Route Display Polish**: Improved multi-turn planning and potion trade-offs in Boss and Elite fights; automatically coordinates offset same-period counter relics (such as Pendulum and Happy Flower) by priority instead of stalling; fixed final-turn projected HP loss display when enemies die from Doom or end-of-turn effects.
- **Full v0.113.1 Compatibility & Sweeping Accuracy Overhaul**: Updated for Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1 across Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes with broad resolution fixes across all 6 characters' cards, relics, powers, potions, and monsters—substantially reducing prediction mismatches and unplanned recalculations during manual and auto-play execution.
