# CombatSolver 0.51.9

## 简体中文

- 特别感谢[苏幽璃](https://github.com/iRyougi)在 [PR #264](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/264) 中为愚者的 18 张单人能力牌逐张优化了出牌判断。使用射箭或旋律牌组时，求解器会考虑先花能量打出能力牌，再靠后续几回合的伤害或格挡减少整场战损。打出「倦怠」前，也会权衡额外抽牌的收益，以及「悲伤」逐回合占用手牌空间的代价。部分长战斗的求解时间会相应增加。

## English

- Special thanks to [苏幽璃 (iRyougi)](https://github.com/iRyougi) for improving how the solver plays all 18 of the Fool’s single-player Powers in [PR #264](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/264). In arrow and melody decks, the solver considers spending Energy on a Power early when the damage or Block it provides over later turns can save HP across the fight. Before playing Ennui, it also weighs the extra draw against the hand space that Sadness takes up each turn. Some longer fights may take more time to solve.
