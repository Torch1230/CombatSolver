# CombatSolver 0.46.0

## 简体中文

- 调整战斗悬浮窗的文字大小和排版，路线、搜索状态与操作按钮更容易辨认；深色和浅色主题的控件样式也更加一致。
- 设置页的分组更清楚，修复部分开关只显示白点的问题；成长与遗物策略中的开关排列更整齐。
- 重复出牌在路线中紧凑成组，次数跟随动作显示；执行完成后整组淡化。每回合的伤害、战损和能量数值也更容易逐行对照。
- 回合开始的选牌步骤现在有独立色标，等待选择和完成后的状态更清楚；补齐这些步骤的中英文来源与操作文字。

## English

- Adjusted text size and layout in the combat overlay so routes, search status, and action buttons are easier to read. Controls are also more consistent across light and dark themes.
- Settings are grouped more clearly. Fixed some switches appearing as white dots and aligned the switches in the Growth and Relic strategy panels.
- Repeated card plays are grouped compactly in the route, with the repeat count beside the actions. The group dims after completion, and each turn's damage, HP loss, and energy figures are easier to compare.
- Turn-start card choices now have their own color marker and clearer waiting and completed states. Their sources and actions have more complete Chinese and English text.
