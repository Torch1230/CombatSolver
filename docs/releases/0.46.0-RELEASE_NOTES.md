# CombatSolver 0.46.0

## 简体中文

- 大幅重做求解器界面：战斗悬浮窗、设置页、策略面板和问题包窗口的文字与控件更统一；减少重复色块、边框和装饰，深浅主题都更整洁易读。
- 设置分组更容易浏览，开关状态更清楚；修复开关偶尔只显示白点的问题，并对齐成长与遗物策略中的开关。
- 路线更容易逐行查看：动作类别改用简明色标，伤害、战损和能量数值排列更整齐；重复出牌的分组更紧凑、与相邻动作对齐，执行完成后随路线淡化。
- 回合开始的选牌步骤有独立标识，等待选择和完成后的状态更容易辨认；补齐这些步骤的中英文来源与操作文字。

## English

- The solver interface has been extensively redesigned. The combat overlay, settings, strategy panels, and bug report dialog now use clearer text and more consistent controls, with fewer decorative colors and borders in both light and dark themes.
- Settings are easier to scan, and switch states are clearer. Fixed switches occasionally appearing as white dots and aligned the switches in the Growth and Relic strategy panels.
- Routes are easier to follow turn by turn: action types use simple color markers, damage, HP loss, and energy figures line up, and repeated card play groups are more compact, align with adjacent actions, and dim when completed.
- Turn-start card choices have their own marker and clearer waiting and completed states, with more complete source and action text in Chinese and English.
