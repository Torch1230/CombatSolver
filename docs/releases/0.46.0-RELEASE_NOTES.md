# CombatSolver 0.46.0

## 简体中文

- 全面重构界面排版与视觉层级：适当放大了字体字阶，去除文字内部发糊的人工暗色描边，并统一中文字体为清晰锐利的思源宋体粗体，在大屏及高分辨率下文字更加清晰易读。
- 设置与策略面板交互规范化：扁平化设置布局并去除多余底框嵌套；彻底修复深浅主题下开关控件偶现渲染成白点的问题；修正成长策略与遗物策略中开关控件的横向对齐。
- 重构路线动作与循环序列呈现：
  - 重复出牌循环改用紧凑轻量的独立卡片组呈现，移除下方的虚线下划线；
  - 循环框内的卡牌与框外相邻卡牌实现像素级绝对平齐，消除卡牌下沉错位；
  - 循环组根据内部卡牌实际宽度紧凑贴合，消除大框右侧的空洞留白；循环次数徽章随卡牌流式排版紧随其后；
  - 循环序列的所有轮次全部打完后，循环徽章与外框随卡牌平滑淡化熄灭，呈现完整的出牌与结束反馈。
- 完善回合开始选牌交互与中英本地化：
  - 工具箱、选择悖论、赌博筹码、必备工具等回合开始选牌胶囊采用专属温暖色标，与普通技能牌明确区分；
  - 完整补齐所有回合开始选牌来源与动作效果的中英文本地化翻译与标点适配；
  - 待选牌期间提供柔和呼吸动画提示，选牌完成开始出牌后平滑淡化熄灭。
- 规范路线指标对齐：每行回合末的对敌伤害、战损与剩余费用采用固定列宽与右对齐，彻底消除跨回合数字位数变化引起的左右晃动。

## English

- Overhauled UI typography and visual hierarchy: Scaled up font sizes, removed blurry artificial text outlines for crisper text rendering, and unified Chinese serif typography, significantly improving readability on 1080p, 1440p, and 4K displays.
- Standardized settings and strategy panels: Flattened settings group layouts to eliminate redundant nested panels; completely resolved toggle switches occasionally rendering as plain white dots across light and dark themes; fixed switch alignments in the Growth and Relic strategy panels.
- Redesigned route action sequences and repeating loops:
  - Repeating action loops are now enclosed in a clean, compact group frame, removing the dashed underlines;
  - Cards inside the loop box are now pixel-perfect baseline-aligned with outside sibling cards, eliminating vertical sinking;
  - The loop box snugly hugs the wrapped cards instead of stretching across the row, and the repetition badge naturally flows right after the last card;
  - After all repetitions of a loop sequence finish playing, the loop badge and outer frame smoothly dim alongside the completed cards.
- Enhanced turn-start choice capsules with full bilingual localization:
  - Choice capsules (such as Toolbox, Choices Paradox, Gambling Chip, Tools of the Trade) now feature a distinctive warm amber color marker to avoid confusion with Skill cards;
  - Fully translated all turn-start choice sources and effect actions with proper bilingual punctuation;
  - Choice capsules gently pulse while awaiting player selection and smoothly dim once play begins.
- Standardized route metrics layout: Enemy damage, HP loss, and remaining energy now use fixed tabular column widths and right alignment, completely preventing horizontal jitter caused by varying digit counts across turns.
