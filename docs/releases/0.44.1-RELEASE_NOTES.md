# CombatSolver 0.44.1

## 简体中文

- 感谢 [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012) 提交的 [PR #126](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/126) 和 [PR #128](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/128)：第三方玩法模组现在可以为回合开始前、玩家抽牌后的回合开始效果提供求解器适配。完成适配的能力、遗物等效果会按游戏顺序参与路线计算；相关模组仍需登记具体效果。
- 感谢 [xhj66](https://github.com/xhj66) 提交的 [PR #127](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/127)：修复击杀敌人后仍预测其承受后续减益，以及能力或遗物触发的效果被误算成当前卡牌施加的问题，减少遗物触发次数、中毒等状态与实战不一致的情况。
- 与 Loadout v0.5.6 同时使用且没有为怪物配置额外能力时，不再因其召唤监听器误报不兼容。已配置怪物能力仍会明确停止路线计算，避免漏算新加入怪物和阶段切换后的能力效果。

## English

- [PR #126](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/126) and [PR #128](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/128) by [s1f102500012](https://github.com/s1f102500012): Gameplay mods can now provide solver adapters for effects before a side's turn starts and after the player draws at turn start. Adapted Powers, relics, and other effects are processed in game order; each mod still needs to register its effects.
- [PR #127](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/pull/127) by [xhj66](https://github.com/xhj66): Fixed predictions that applied debuffs to enemies already removed by a killing blow, and effects from Powers or relics being mistaken for effects applied by the current card. This reduces mismatches in relic triggers, Poison, and related combat state.
- Fixed an erroneous incompatibility report for Loadout v0.5.6 when no extra monster Powers are configured. Configured monster Powers still stop route calculation because their effects on newly added monsters and phase transitions are not yet simulated.
