# CombatSolver 0.36.5

## 简体中文

- 改善部分三层首领战的能力铺垫和连续出牌选择，让一些需要先投入资源、再发挥收益的路线更容易被找到。
- 改善持续增伤、抽牌与能量之间的联动判断，以及首张攻击增幅与多段攻击的配合。
- 修正召唤物承伤后溢出伤害与有限次数免伤的预估，减少对安全铺垫机会的误判。
- 移除普通、精英和首领战的旧累计卖血硬限制，允许搜索回血后继续投入血量的可生存路线。
- 保留原有整场战损比较、药水保护与强制使用、成长、遗物目标和达标早停设置。

本次改善了部分实际反馈中的路线，尚不能保证所有复杂首领局面都能找到最优解。

## English

- Improved setup and follow-up choices in some Act 3 boss fights, helping the solver find lines that invest resources before their benefits become available.
- Improved evaluation of recurring damage, draw and energy interactions, including first-attack bonuses with multi-hit attacks.
- Corrected threat estimates involving summon damage overflow and limited-use damage prevention, reducing missed opportunities for safe setup.
- Removed the old cumulative HP-investment caps in normal, elite and boss fights, allowing survivable lines that heal and then invest HP again.
- Preserved full-combat loss comparisons, potion protection and forced use, growth and relic goals, and target-based early stopping.

This update improves some reported fights; it does not guarantee an optimal solution for every complex boss encounter.
