# CombatSolver 0.51.1 · 0.113 适配 Part 2

## 简体中文

本次更新适配《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.0 Beta 的单人恐惧与极速模式，并修复新版战斗中发现的问题。

请同时更新 RitsuLib 至 0.6.11 或更高版本。

- 新增六位角色在恐惧与极速模式下的战斗路线求解，支持单步执行、连续自动执行、跨回合续用、路线缓存及问题包恢复。
- 恐惧模式适配全部 12 种领域、领域带来的开局选择与增援效果，以及首领和衍生怪物的强化。
- 极速模式适配 0–5 级进阶规则、随机生成与变牌范围，以及怪物行为变化；智能药水策略结合该模式的战后药水奖励，评估满槽时使用药水腾出位置的收益。
- 修复部分附魔牌导致路线计算失败、连续报错的问题。
- 修复遭遇虔诚雕刻师时无法开始计算路线的问题。
- 修复音乐盒复制牌及谋划专家赋予奇巧后的路线失配，避免找不到计划卡牌或跨回合重复计算。
- 修复狂喜在手牌中没有悲伤时引发的后续随机结果偏差与路线重算。
- 修复永世沙漏加入枯萎后的牌序与洗牌预测偏差，改善后续回合的路线续用。

恐惧与极速模式的战绩统计和精彩战斗采集将在后续版本扩展。

## English

This update adds support for single-player Dread and Blitz modes in Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.0 Beta and fixes issues found in the new version's combat.

Please also update RitsuLib to version 0.6.11 or later.

- Added combat route solving for all six characters in Dread and Blitz modes, including step-by-step execution, continuous auto-play, continuation across turns, route caching, and issue-bundle restoration.
- Added support for all 12 Dread Dominions, their opening choices and reinforcement effects, and stronger bosses and their associated monsters.
- Added support for Blitz Ascension levels 0–5, its random card generation and transformation pools, and changed monster behavior. Smart potion strategy accounts for Blitz's post-combat potion rewards when evaluating the benefit of freeing a full potion slot.
- Fixed certain enchanted cards causing route calculation to fail and errors to repeat.
- Fixed route calculation failing to start in Devoted Sculptor encounters.
- Fixed route mismatches involving Music Box copies and skills given Sly by Master Planner, preventing missing planned cards and repeated recalculation across turns.
- Fixed Ecstasy consuming randomness when no Sadness was in hand, which could change later random outcomes and trigger recalculation.
- Fixed card order and shuffle predictions after Aeonglass adds Wither, improving route continuation on later turns.

Run statistics and combat highlight collection for Dread and Blitz will be expanded in a later update.
