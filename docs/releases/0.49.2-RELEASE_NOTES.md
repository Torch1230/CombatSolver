# CombatSolver 0.49.2

## 简体中文

相对于 0.49.1 的更新：

- 扩展对未适配内容 Mod 的识别，涵盖 BetterVanillaSTS2、第三方怪物和选牌扩展等情况。遇到已确认的内容兼容问题时，提示“求解器暂未适配此内容性 Mod：名称，无法求解。”，显示所属 Mod 名称，并停止求解；这类问题不再引导上传日志。
- 修复安装部分内容 Mod 后，效果预加载引起的求解启动失败。
- 修正运行环境异常的报错分类，普通计算失败保留问题包上传提示，便于排查。
- 修复部分移动端环境因内存管理不兼容导致的计算失败。
- 修复虚空形态强制结束回合后，知识恶魔及下一回合的选牌可能执行失败的问题。
- 修复群体减益触发凶恶、地狱狂徒并击杀敌人时，后续结算导致计算失败的问题。
- 修复玩家死亡后的金币结算可能导致计算失败的问题。
- 修复回合准备阶段重新计算路线时，漏算下一回合额外能量的问题。
- 修复镀金缆线与等离子的回合开始能量预测错误。
- 修复忍术卷轴首回合生成的小刀被漏算，导致预测手牌不完整的问题。
- 修复佩尔之眼与彼岸咆哮同时存在时，回合末自动出牌及额外回合的结算顺序错误。
- 修复瀑布巨兽改变行动后，眼部攻击的虚弱效果预测错误。
- 修复实战推进或结束后，路线中的能量与抽牌数可能预测错误的问题。
- 修复跨回合自动执行时，上回合进度覆盖新路线并导致界面报错的问题。
- 修复铁斩波、武装和挑衅的格挡被反伤消耗后，重复计算格挡，导致战损预测偏低的问题。

已知问题：个别缩小甲虫战斗的求解启动失败仍在排查。

## English

Changes since 0.49.1:

- Expanded detection of unsupported content mods, including BetterVanillaSTS2, third-party monsters and card-choice extensions. Confirmed content compatibility failures identify the mod, state that the solver does not yet support it, and stop solving. These failures no longer prompt players to upload logs.
- Fixed solver startup failures caused by preloading effects from some content mods.
- Corrected error classification for runtime failures. Ordinary calculation failures retain diagnostic upload prompts to aid investigation.
- Fixed calculation failures caused by incompatible memory management on some mobile runtimes.
- Fixed card-choice execution failures involving Knowledge Demon and the next player turn after Void Form forces the turn to end.
- Fixed calculation failures when area debuffs trigger Vicious and Hellraiser, kill an enemy and continue resolving effects.
- Fixed gold settlement after the player's death potentially causing calculation failures.
- Fixed extra energy for the next turn being omitted when recalculating a route during turn setup.
- Fixed turn-start energy predictions for Gold-Plated Cables with Plasma.
- Fixed missing first-turn Shivs from Ninja Scroll leaving the predicted hand incomplete.
- Fixed the resolution order of end-of-turn automatic plays and extra turns when Pael's Eye and Howl from Beyond are both present.
- Fixed Go for the Eyes predicting Weak incorrectly after Waterfall Giant changes its move.
- Fixed route energy and draw predictions being affected by the live battle advancing or ending.
- Fixed progress updates from the previous turn overwriting the new route and causing interface errors during automatic execution.
- Fixed block from Iron Wave, Armaments and Taunt being counted twice after retaliation damage consumes it, causing predicted HP loss to be too low.

Known issue: solver startup failures in some Shrinker Beetle battles remain under investigation.
