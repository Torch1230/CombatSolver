# CombatSolver 1.0.2

## 简体中文

CombatSolver 1.0.2 在 1.0.1 引擎的基础上重点提升复杂战斗中的路线规划效率与胜率稳定性，并进一步清零高风险场景下的结算偏差与意外重算，支持《杀戮尖塔 2》v0.113.1 单人标准、恐惧与极速模式（兼容 0.113.0）。请确保同时启用 RitsuLib 0.6.11 或更高版本。

- **路线规划提速与长线首领胜率提升**：优化多阶段搜索的时间与预算分配，加强险胜、残血逃生与药水保命路径的保留权重，并根据角色最大生命值动态权衡血液药水的使用成本；常见对局平均求解耗时显著降低，多场复杂首领战也能更稳定地减少战损并保住残局胜势。
- **修复特定牌组循环导致的计算停滞**：解决部分高风险战斗中连续抽牌、弃牌或自动打出牌链（如多次衍生触发）陷入死循环的问题，避免战斗求解长时间停滞或界面无响应。
- **进一步清零高风险战斗预测偏差与中途重算**：结合高风险模拟与实战复现日志，系统修正全部 6 位角色的数十项实战规则细节——包含升级降费时钳制超限的固定费用修改效果、消耗牌被遗物复制时保留临时减费、由药水或开局效果自动打出结束回合牌时正确切换回合、目标先于攻击被击杀后仍正确消耗活力层数、手牌回合末效果在最大手牌数与抽牌触发前的结算时机、孵化或复活类怪物的减益保留与眩晕复活衔接、衍生物牌池独立变形，以及部分遗物／能力（如仪式兽、交易工具、暴君等）的触发顺序，进一步清零高风险对局中的计划外重算与选牌错位，同时保留极少数多层复活敌人对局中药水取舍的后续优化空间。

## English

CombatSolver 1.0.2 builds on 1.0.1 with faster combat route planning, stronger survival stability in demanding fights, and further elimination of high-risk combat prediction mismatches and unplanned replans. It supports single-player Standard, Dread, and Blitz modes in Slay the Spire 2 v0.113.1 (compatible with 0.113.0). Please ensure RitsuLib 0.6.11 or later is enabled.

- **Faster Route Search & Stronger Endurance in Tough Boss Fights**: Refined multi-stage search pacing and budget retention—protecting low-HP survival lines and near-death escapes while scaling Blood Potion cost evaluation with your max HP. Typical solves complete noticeably faster while preserving healthier HP totals and avoiding fatal turns in complex Boss encounters.
- **Eliminated Infinite-Loop Stalls in Complex Card Chains**: Resolved loop edge cases across repeated draw, discard, and auto-play chains that could stall route calculation or leave the combat UI unresponsive in high-risk decks.
- **Further Reduction in High-Risk Mismatches & Mid-Combat Replans**: Based on high-risk simulations and live combat logs across all 6 characters, resolved dozens of combat-rule edge cases—including clamping temporary fixed-cost modifiers on cost-reducing upgrades, preserving temporary cost reductions on exhaust clones, honoring end-turn triggers when auto-played by potions or turn-start effects, consuming attack modifiers consistently even when pre-hit triggers defeat the target, ordering turn-end hand triggers before max-hand-size draw checks, preserving debuffs across hatches and stunned-revive transitions, isolating token transform pools, and aligning timing for powers/relics such as Ceremonial Beast, Tools of the Trade, and Tyranny—further eliminating unplanned replans and selection misalignments, while leaving minor potion trade-off tuning room in rare multi-segment reviving fights.
