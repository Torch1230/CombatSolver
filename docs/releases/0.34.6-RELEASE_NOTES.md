# CombatSolver 0.34.6 试玩版

## 简体中文

- 成长策略侧栏新增四种搜索目标：平衡、保命优先、永久培养优先、净收益优先。
- 收益模式可设置本场累计战损上限和最低结束血量；永久培养可设置本次路线的成长点数目标，达到后优先减少战损和回合数。重新搜索时重新计量。
- 路线显示永久最大生命、伤害和格挡成长，以及金币、待结算金币、药水净变化与额外选牌奖励。净收益分是规则估值，不代表售价、胜率或整局最优收益。
- 没有找到满足收益限制的完整胜利路线时，全自动暂停，交还玩家判断。
- 战后奖励与商店改为显示一位小数的评分，越高越优先。跳过/留钱为 0 分，商店评分计入价格；通用估值标为参考分。

## English

- The growth sidebar now offers four search objectives: Balanced, Survival, Permanent Growth, and Net Resources.
- Reward objectives support total battle HP-loss and ending-HP limits. Permanent Growth can target a number of points for the current route; once reached, lower HP loss and fewer turns take priority. A new search resets this measurement.
- Routes show permanent max HP, damage and block gains, net gold, pending gold, potion changes and extra card rewards. Resource scores are heuristic estimates, not prices, win rates or whole-run optimality guarantees.
- Full auto pauses if no complete winning route satisfying the reward limits has been found.
- Card rewards and shop offers now show scores with one decimal place. Higher is better; skipping/saving is worth zero. Shop scores include price, and generic evaluations are labelled as estimates.
