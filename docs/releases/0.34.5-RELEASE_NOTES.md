# 0.34.5 — 奖励与商店建议（本地试玩）

- 战后选牌显示推荐顺序和简短理由，“跳过”也参与比较。
- 商店显示卡牌、遗物、药水和删牌的购买优先级，考虑当前金币，购买后自动更新。
- 删牌建议显示优先移除的牌；金币不足或药水栏已满时给出提示。
- 部分商品显示低置信度的“参考”排名，未知第三方效果显示“暂不评级”。建议基于规则，不保证整局最优，也不会替你点击购买。
- 支持中英文显示，保留现有战斗求解功能。

# English

- Reward cards and skipping show priorities with short reasons.
- Shops compare cards, relics, potions, removal and saving gold, refreshing after purchases.
- Removal names the preferred card; insufficient gold and full potion slots are indicated.
- Generic estimates are marked separately, and unknown third-party effects remain unrated. These are heuristic suggestions, not an optimal-run guarantee or automatic purchases.
- Chinese and English display are supported; existing combat solving remains available.
