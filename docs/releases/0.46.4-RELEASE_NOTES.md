# CombatSolver 0.46.4

## 简体中文

- 感谢 [fdkgf](https://github.com/fdkgf) 在 [issue #135](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/issues/135) 提供的优化思路：搜索在确认后续没有可用治疗时，会更早排除已无法改善战损的失血路线。带治疗牌、药水或其他未覆盖回血来源的路线继续保留；精神过载在后续回合施加的灾厄仍到该回合结束才判定生死，允许玩家先行获胜。

## English

- Thanks to [fdkgf](https://github.com/fdkgf) for the idea in [issue #135](https://github.com/Torch1230/CombatSolver/issues/135): Search can retire HP-loss routes earlier when it can establish that no later healing is available. Routes with healing cards, potions, or other unclassified recovery sources remain eligible. Neurosurge applies Doom on a later turn, and its lethal check remains at that turn's end, leaving time to win first.
