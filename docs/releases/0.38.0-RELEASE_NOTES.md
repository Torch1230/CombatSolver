# CombatSolver 0.38.0：计划外重算与卡牌估值修复 / Replan and Card Evaluation Fixes

## 简体中文

本次更新修正多种卡牌、遗物和怪物效果的预测偏差，减少由这些偏差引起的计划外重算，并改善环绕轨道和自动化的使用判断。

### 环绕轨道与自动化

- 改善环绕轨道的长期收益判断，将后续花费能量带来的返能计入评价，减少长战中忽视这张牌的情况。
- 改善自动化的长期收益判断，包含自然抽牌带来的返能：每回合自然抽5张牌时，单份效果每两回合可返还1费，这部分持续收益也纳入评价。
- 修复已有环绕轨道时重新计算路线，丢失已累计花费能量的问题，避免返能时点预测错误。
- 修复环绕轨道在“不能获得能量”的效果生效时，仍被预测为可以返能的问题；解除限制后正常继续累计和触发。
- 修复多次打出自动化或滚石时，各份效果被错误合并的问题，保留各自的计数、成长和触发。

### 卡牌结算与选牌

- 修复战利品的铸造与抽牌顺序：先生成君王之剑，再抽牌。手牌将满时，正确判断君王之剑占位后还能抽几张牌。
- 修复肾上腺素、祭品和精神过载抽到虚空时多算1费的问题，按原版先返能、再抽牌；祭品的扣血也在抽牌前结算。
- 修复夜魇与无尽刀刃、哨卫模式等效果同时存在时，回合开始生成牌的顺序错误；已有夜魇效果时重新计算，也会保留其选中的卡牌。
- 修复隐秘匕首等效果必须全选、原版自动省略选牌界面时，求解器擅自调换所选卡牌顺序的问题。
- 修复坚定不移把余像等非卡牌格挡误算为卡牌格挡次数的问题，避免后续卡牌的格挡加倍机会提前消耗。
- 修复蜃景在没有中毒目标、基础格挡为0时，漏算敏捷带来的格挡的问题。
- 修复回合准备中已经获得星能时，辐射的攻击次数被低估的问题。
- 修复致死性已经用于痛殴本次攻击后，在吸收另一张手牌的伤害时再次套用首攻加成的问题。
- 修复小刀被抑制降级后，幻影之刃在预测中错误地重新补回保留的问题。
- 修复幽灵种子赋予的虚无被降级清除后，预测中仍反复补回虚无的问题。
- 修复爪击和巨镰击杀抑制施法者时的结算顺序，先恢复升级、再计算卡牌成长，避免少算成长值。

### 遗物、怪物与自动执行

- 修复伤害穿透格挡、随后被缓冲抵消时，漏算情感芯片下回合触发的问题。
- 修复王之凝视附带的减力量效果错误消耗不安油灯的预测，避免影响之后直接施加的减益。
- 修复黄金印的扣金币数值为负数时，漏算其实际增加金币的问题。
- 修复招架盾等效果对熟睡甲虫、乐加维林族母造成伤害后，唤醒状态更新不及时的问题。
- 修正组装师召唤物入场时，贤者之石等效果与爪牙的生效顺序。
- 修复帝皇蟹同伴被旋风斩等多段攻击击杀后，存活同伴的力量和格挡更新过晚，导致后续伤害预测偏高的问题。
- 修复全自动已开始或已完成本回合部署后，迟到的自动请求再次触发计算的问题，减少无意义的重复重算。

## English

This update corrects prediction errors involving cards, relics, and enemies, reducing unexpected replans caused by those errors. It also improves decisions about playing Orbit and Automation.

### Orbit and Automation

- Improved Orbit's long-term evaluation by accounting for energy refunded from future spending, making it less likely to be overlooked in longer fights.
- Improved Automation's long-term evaluation to include normal turn-start draws. With five natural draws per turn, one instance refunds one energy every two turns; this recurring benefit is now included in its evaluation.
- Fixed replanning with an active Orbit losing the energy already spent toward its next refund, which could predict refunds at the wrong time.
- Fixed Orbit being predicted to refund energy while an effect prevents energy gain. Spending continues to count, and refunds resume normally after the restriction ends.
- Fixed repeated plays of Automation or Rolling Boulder incorrectly merging their effects. Each instance now keeps its own counters, growth, and triggers.

### Card resolution and selection

- Fixed Spoils of Battle's Forge and draw order: Sovereign Blade is created before drawing. Near the hand limit, predictions now account for the slot taken by the Blade.
- Fixed Adrenaline, Offering, and Neurosurge overestimating remaining energy by one when drawing Void. Energy is gained before drawing, as in the game; Offering's HP loss also resolves before the draw.
- Fixed turn-start card generation order when Nightmare is combined with effects such as Infinite Blades or Sentry Mode. Replanning with an active Nightmare also preserves its selected card.
- Fixed effects such as Hidden Daggers reordering selected cards when all eligible cards must be selected and the game skips the selection screen.
- Fixed Unmovable counting non-card block gains, such as Afterimage, toward its card block count, which could consume a later card's doubled-block opportunity too early.
- Fixed Mirage missing Dexterity's block bonus when there are no poisoned targets and its base block is zero.
- Fixed Radiate underestimating its attack count when Stars had already been gained during turn setup.
- Fixed Lethality's first-attack bonus being applied again when Thrash absorbs another card's damage after that bonus has already been used for the current attack.
- Fixed predictions incorrectly restoring Retain from Phantom Blades after Dampen downgrades a Shiv and removes that keyword.
- Fixed predictions repeatedly restoring Ethereal from Ghost Seed after a downgrade removes it.
- Fixed Claw and The Scythe resolving their growth before upgrades were restored on killing Dampen's caster, which could underestimate their growth.

### Relics, enemies, and automatic execution

- Fixed Emotion Chip's next-turn trigger being missed when damage passes through Block but is then prevented by Buffer.
- Fixed Monarch's Gaze's incidental Strength reduction incorrectly consuming Unsettling Lamp in predictions, affecting later directly applied debuffs.
- Fixed Seal of Gold's coin gain being missed when its gold-loss amount is negative.
- Fixed delayed wake-up state updates when Slumbering Beetle or Lagavulin Matriarch takes damage from effects such as Parrying Shield.
- Corrected the order of entry effects, including Philosopher's Stone, and Minion on Fabricator's summons.
- Fixed surviving Kaiser Crab allies gaining Strength and Block too late after an ally is killed during a multi-hit attack such as Whirlwind, which could overestimate subsequent damage.
- Fixed late automatic requests starting another calculation after the current turn's automatic deployment had already started or finished.
