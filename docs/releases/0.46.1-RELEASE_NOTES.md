# CombatSolver 0.46.1

## 简体中文

- 修复 0.46.0 停止向监控站上报在线状态的问题。更新后，开启在线统计的玩家会重新显示在监控站，新产生的战斗统计和符合条件的战斗展示也会正常上传。

## English

- Fixed online status updates stopping in 0.46.0. After updating, players with online statistics enabled will appear on the monitoring site again, and new combat statistics and eligible combat showcases will upload normally.
