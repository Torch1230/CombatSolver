# 0.36.2 紧急修复 / Hotfix

## 简体中文

紧急修复：解决自动出牌中途误重算、执行中断，以及附毒结算偏差导致的重算问题。

- 修复攻击附带中毒被误算成卡牌直接施加，造成遗物翻倍、敌方生命预测错误及下一回合重算的问题。
- 第三方卡牌的计算效果不受支持时，明确提示对应 Mod 不兼容，不再要求玩家上传日志。

- 修复自动出牌过程中，重复的回合开始通知误触发重算、清空路线并中断执行的问题。手动重新计算仍正常可用。
- 围巾触发的第五张免费牌，现在会在动作胶囊中显示遗物名称与“本张免费”，方便确认触发时机。

## English

Hotfix: addresses unnecessary recalculation and interrupted auto-play, plus replanning caused by incorrect on-hit poison predictions.

- Fixed on-hit poison being treated as a direct card effect, incorrectly triggering relic amplification and causing wrong enemy HP predictions and next-turn replanning.
- Unsupported calculated effects on third-party cards now identify the incompatible mod without asking players to upload logs.

- Fixed duplicate turn-start requests triggering unnecessary recalculation during auto-play, clearing the route and interrupting execution. Manual recalculation remains available.
- The fifth card made free by Brilliant Scarf now shows the relic name and “Free card” in its action capsule.
