# CombatSolver 0.43.1

## 简体中文

- 修复游戏使用英文等非中文语言时，战斗路线悬浮窗可能完全不显示、快捷键也无法唤出的问题。药水、成长和遗物面板的“收起”按钮现在会正确显示为当前语言。
- 成长策略增加“疯狂科学”：事件获得的能力牌若带有“改进”效果，可以单独设置愿意为战后永久升级承担的战损；其他效果的疯狂科学不会被计为成长。

## English

- Fixed the combat overlay failing to appear, with its shortcut unable to open it, when the game uses English or another non-Chinese language. The potion, growth, and relic panels now show a localized “Collapse” button.
- Added Mad Science to Growth Strategy: its Power card with the Improvement effect has a separate HP-loss allowance for permanent deck upgrades. Other Mad Science variants do not count as growth.
